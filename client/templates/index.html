MONITORING / WATER TESTING

Water quality testing

Measure the conditions of your water and compare them with saved fish requirements.

i

Sensor-aware results. Readings are shown as received. Scans collect samples for 3–5 seconds, reject isolated spikes, and report when stability cannot be established. MQ-137 measures ammonia gas; it is excluded from water suitability scoring until a separate gas-risk calibration is validated.
